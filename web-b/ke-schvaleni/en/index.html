Technology and build quality

B

Building energy rating from the Czech energy performance certificate.

Included in the base price at handover

Heating
Underfloor heating runs through all three levels. A gas boiler is the main heat source. If you add a heat pump, the boiler stays in place as a backup.
Ventilation
A Licon heat recovery unit sits in a recess in the garage. The price includes ductwork and air outlets on all three levels.
Home controls
The handover includes a basic Loxone smart home system and a video intercom at the gate with an indoor monitor.
Thermal storage
The underground thermal store beneath the villa is included. Once solar PV panels and a heat pump are installed, it can be connected to heat the domestic hot water.
